About Me · Business Analyst

Understanding business needs. Shaping practical solutions.

I'm Nandini Jain, a Business Analyst with experience translating business objectives into functional requirements and actionable product solutions. I work at the intersection of business and technology, helping keep stakeholder expectations, user needs, and development priorities aligned.

My work spans stakeholder management, requirement elicitation and analysis, backlog refinement and prioritization, user story creation, acceptance criteria definition, functional solution design and documentation, and process modelling. I also contribute to sprint planning, Agile ceremonies, and cross-functional discussions that support effective delivery.

I enjoy understanding the problem behind a request, asking the questions needed to clarify it, and breaking it down into requirements that teams can discuss, estimate, and implement. I aim to bring structure to ambiguity while keeping the business objective and end-user needs in view.

Alongside my Business Analysis work, I am building my data analytics capabilities through hands-on Power BI projects. This helps me explore how KPIs, trends, and reporting can support clearer operational and business decisions.

My approach

From problem understanding to delivery clarity

Understand the context

Understand the business objective, stakeholder expectations, users, existing processes and the underlying problem before defining a solution.

Structure the requirements

Elicit and analyse requirements, identify gaps, map workflows and translate business needs into clear, documented requirements.

Enable aligned delivery

Facilitate discussions, refine the backlog, define user stories and acceptance criteria, and maintain a shared understanding between stakeholders and delivery teams.

Validate the outcome

Conduct UAT and evaluate whether the delivered solution addresses the original business need and achieves the intended business outcomes.
